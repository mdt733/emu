# EMU v5019 — two forward ToFs and a shared rear-ToF V memory

**Branch** `feature/v5019-forward-tof-profile`, isolated from ongoing v5018 training. **Do not touch v5018 runs, main, champion policy or Pages.** No new reward, campaign or curriculum governor.

## Physical design

- Two **single-point** forward/downward ToF modules at approximate torso body mounts `(+3.5cm, ±5cm, -2.5cm)`, each nominally aimed at +20cm on flat ground.
- Existing third **rear downward tripod ToF**, calibrated at body mount `(-4cm, 0, -2.5cm)`, forms the junction of two V-shaped ground tracks. The front-right/front-left downward ToFs still help estimate the local tripod plane. Actual mounts are provisional.
- An optical-flow + gyro-compensated IMU velocity estimate and measured IMU yaw rate transport remembered measurements during every 20ms PPO control step. Exactly **two** new forward laser returns are acquired every 40ms when valid, plus the **shared rear anchor**. Delayed returns are inserted once; a failed return is not faked as flat.
- **Geometry follows measurements:** each incoming slant range, tripod plane and IMU gravity supplies a measured local hit X/Y/relative height. Torso pitch, roll and vertical height move laser-hit coordinates; the rear junction is recomputed from the **actual delayed rear-ToF hit** rather than treating −5cm as a world-fixed point.
- **Sparse spatial memory:** 51 × 2 cells, 1cm apart, from **−10cm through +40cm** relative to torso heading. The ±Y branches meet at the rear measured point, spread toward ±5cm at the forward calibration point (+20cm), and continue out as approximate V arms. Backward walking is supported. Turning transforms new V coordinates into the previous map using measured `vx/vy/yaw`; right and left memories can therefore move in opposite directions. Confidence falls when the new path turns or translates away from previously observed ground.
- **Inputs to PPO:** signed relative heights at **−5/0/+5/+10/+15cm** along *each V branch*, plus separate confidence at each of those 10 points. Original 58 inputs + 10 heights + 10 confidences = 78 per frame, with 4-frame history = **312 inputs** (`rolling_tof_312`). Six servo outputs unchanged. The **old 220 and 232 contracts/checkpoint states remain distinct**; the new schema-9 fingerprint changes with geometry/spacing/semantics.
- A matched `--forward-preview-masked` training baseline keeps exactly 312 dimensions and physics, but zeros the 20 new actor fields. The existing read-only physical step examiner also accepts `--ablate-forward-preview` for freezing a trained sighted actor and observing what happens when its memory is blanked.

## Limitations that matter

This is a **best-effort V-shaped memory, not a real terrain map**. A robot yawing sharply may later tread off the scanned paths; lateral confidence weighting reduces trust, not magical reconstruction. Pitch/roll body-to-gravity projection, range noise, sensor timing and estimated flow errors make remembered points approximate. The rear anchor provides **the local height reference and new physical ground point**, but it cannot recover world-absolute elevation from one range measurement. It does not fix temporal changes in terrain reference across large steps; those observations decay.

Narrow rays can miss risers. Box+plane intersections are implemented; logs, ramps, hfields, robot-leg occlusion, reflectance and an installed hardware mount are not yet modeled. Browser policy rendering still cannot generate a 312-input observation: Python checkpoint/export/exam support is not browser implementation. No policy has been trained on these 312 inputs.

**Completion gate before GPU learning:** show physically different measured ranges when a known collidable step enters view; show +20cm returns advect inward toward +15cm under ~5cm estimated forward motion; show the rear reading genuinely seeds both V arms at its measured coordinate; verify backward motion/heading turns reduce or transform confidence instead of creating false terrain. Then, after studying the current v5018 run, compare one short **same-312-width sighted versus masked** learning pair for held-out 20–40mm steps and flat-ground regression. No extra forks or governors.

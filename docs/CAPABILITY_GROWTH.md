## The mission (do not drift)

Grow **the range and quality of situations in which one cold-start EMU policy can follow its commands, remain stable, recover, and move with mechanical grace**. A better result is a wider *reliably competent* capability envelope, not one highest speed, step height or reward.

One physical world → physically plausible sensors → actor → PPO → reward teacher → independent examiner → evidenced capability map → one curriculum director. Keep their responsibilities separate. Retain the accepted PPO, rewards, curriculum, capability field, evaluations, training health, rollback, checkpoint and resume machinery of `main` unless a measured issue requires a reviewed change.

### Rules for every capability

1. **Physical definition:** identify the variable, units, limits and *actual* effect on collision, forces, commands or sensor measurements. A labelled "step" with no MJX contact is not a step.
2. **Actor honesty:** only realistic, delayed/noisy measured inputs enter the actor. Truth about future terrain, precise foot identity, contacts and task success belongs exclusively to teaching/diagnostics.
3. **Teaching:** preserve command-following and locomotion reward while teaching supported progress; don't let circling, reversing, freezing or falling count as a completed crossing. Safe refusal/avoidance is a separate future commanded behaviour.
4. **Evidence:** separate success (completion, support, accuracy), grace (impact, slip, smoothness, effort) and confidence (enough independent examples). Task success and safety gate efficiency; low power from doing nothing is not success.
5. **Growth/retreat:** earn small frontier increases through demonstrated performance, sufficient samples and healthy PPO/core performance; retreat failed dimensions quickly. Use a **shared expansion budget**, with local retreat where possible and global intervention for core collapse.
6. **Coverage:** keep steady/transition/resilience/posture and diverse vx/vy/yaw commands in the mix while adding terrain. All training-exposure allocations sum to a bounded budget. Most lessons remain ordinary walking until competence justifies wider coverage.
7. **Truthful examinations:** score fixed *held-out* difficulties not controlled by the training frontier; publish curves plus a quality-aware coverage summary, never hide weak dimensions inside an average. Exams must not directly teach or advance curriculum.
8. **Safe evolution:** cold start remains possible on a new PC. Version observation/physics/checkpoint contracts. Same seed and model size for A/B arms, preserve failure visibility and true resume lineage, never overwrite champion/main as a shortcut.

### Capability families

| Family | Expandable variables | Mandatory evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commands | forward/reverse vx, vy, yaw, combinations | tracking, progress, support, falls |
| Changes | acceleration, deceleration, reversals, command hold/ramp | overshoot, recovery time, falls |
| Resilience | push size/direction/duration; perturbation at birth | regain requested movement, not mere survival |
| Posture | chassis pitch/height and foot-midpoint attitude | body stability and command tracking |
| Continuous ground | uphill/downhill/side slope, crest, changes of grade | same-world collision, traction, tracking |
| Discontinuous ground | step up/down, ledge/log/gap, relative height/width, approach direction | physically pass requested corridor alive and supported |
| Sensor uncertainty | noise, lag, dropout and self-occlusion | stable behaviour, realistic return distribution |
| Grace / hardware | actuator force/shaft-work proxies, slip, impact, jerk, safety | cost **conditional on actual task progress**, separately reported |

Do NOT make a dense eight-dimensional grid. Preserve the existing command/recovery/transition capability field. Add sparse conditional context slices (ground category/severity and quality) only after evidence and runtime measurements justify their value.

### Foundation and lifecycle

- The **first 20M environment steps** follow `main`'s foundation on physically flat ground. No early real steps, including buried obstacles accidentally producing contact or ToF returns. Existing command/frontier, posture, resilience and health training remains enabled. The optical sensor may observe flat ground and moving legs if comparing from cold start, but its semantics must be truthful.
- Step/slope lessons open **no earlier than 20M AND only after core competence passes**. Frontier height/angle and exposure share begin small, increase by evidence and retreat promptly. Preserve easy-world practice throughout. A failed step must not automatically lower a healthy unrelated vx frontier.
- Stronger combined challenges appear only after their component skills are measured. Do not increase all axes simultaneously. Later add safety/refusal as an explicitly commanded higher-level choice, not a PPO loophole.
- Choose a champion on independent capability coverage, quality and weak-area regressions. No policy promotion from a positive training reward alone.
- Prefer one 20M cold-start foundation **forked into same-size visible/blinded terrain-learning arms** to isolate sensor benefit. Preserve optimizer, normalization and curriculum lineage on fork; new observation field values can be introduced gradually without changing width.

## Proposed multi-skill campaign after the first 20M foundation (2026-10-24)

**Do not treat these milestones as required step quotas.** Continue only while held-out capabilities improve; every fork is from a validated, SHA-compatible checkpoint. The currently running 2048-world `terrain_360` foundation is pinned to its **original source commit**. Changes to log display or curriculum on the remote branch are **not** available inside that live process and MUST NOT be pulled into its checkpoint/resume lineage without reviewed compatibility/migration. `main` and champion remain untouched.

| Stage (illustrative cumulative steps) | What actually trains | Independent decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–20M, existing run | Cold-start 360-input actor; fully dormant physical platforms; basic commands, gait, posture, small disturbances | Flat survival, speed tracking (not just speed), normalizer/finite PPO state |
| 20–40M, first continuation | The SAME PPO/actor; stronger vX/vY/yaw/transition/resilience commands, mostly easy births, and evidence-gated ~1cm/5% real steps | Core gait and tracking must not regress before any terrain frontier grows |
| 40–70M, **small matched forks** | Try a no-motor-guidance control vs a mild servo-effort teaching arm (existing `motor_guidance_weight`), and/or a lower terrain ceiling; don't turn every dial simultaneously | Compare same held-out flat and step worlds, fall/survival, tracking, real crossing, motor shaft work/forward metre and slips. Select Pareto-improving arm, not highest reward |
| 70–140M, selected winners | Grow validated step ceiling toward 4cm and diversified commanded motions/recoveries; preserve >=75% ordinary terrain-free lesson births under current governor | Separate step-up/down/angle, commands, disturbance and mechanical-grace evidence; retreat the failing dimension |
| Future mixed-ground branch | **Engineering prerequisite**: genuinely inclined MJX floor and finite steps must coexist, with correct actor ray intersections and correct contact/height rewards. Current v5023 explicitly rejects the combination. Build a new versioned physical contract before continuation/migration and then introduce slopes, transitions, irregular steps and compound demands | Matched, held-out slope/step and combined-condition exams against champion and prior policy; no unreviewed cross-source checkpoint loading |
| Final promotion | Independent capability map: commands, slopes, resilience, steps, smoothness, effort and uncertainty | No major core regressions, credible lower-bound support for claimed new skills and explicit user approval for main/champion |

**Single director, not curriculum soup.** Orthogonal terrain context is sampled on top of existing steady/transition/resilience and posture commands. Each hard dimension has a local exposure/severity frontier and retreats if evidence deteriorates; reserve ordinary gait and simple tasks so new step prowess does not erase mature walking. Add compound lessons only after isolated components are competent, never as a new parallel trainer. Cap new difficulty with a **shared** growth budget and robust checkpoint rollback.

**Grace economics:** current per-frame MJX telemetry already exposes actuator positive shaft power, brake power, torque pressure and stored spring energy. New **console-only** `shaft≈J/m` uses positive mechanical work divided by measured net forward displacement for forward-commanded samples; a near-stationary rollout displays an unknown marker. It is **not** battery energy, a reward component or a thermal model; use the existing opt-in small motor guidance as a controlled A/B reward experiment, and never optimise J/m in isolation from successful walking. Train on task success first, then get more graceful.

**Readable log policy:** one summary line with steps, reward, episode length, fall rate, wall FPS, commanded vx min/max frontier, actual/command vx, physical slope ceiling, **maximum** sampled step height and exposure, resilience curriculum scalar and approximate mechanical J/m. Full numerical diagnostics/health remain in the persisted console and metrics.csv. Foundation step/slope should both say 0, since lift-gait `step_evidence` is not obstacle height.

## Code-first working style (explicit owner priority, 2026-10-23)

**Clean, comprehensible, well-connected code takes priority over test volume.**
Use the current code, not passing test counts, as the starting point for each session.
Before adding another class, config flag, training arm, or v-numbered module, trace the full path from **physical world → sensor → observation → PPO reward → evaluation → curriculum → checkpoint** and decide whether existing owners can be extended simply.

- Prefer one readable path over multiple parallel frameworks, forwarding wrappers, compatibility layers or oversized orchestration.
- Keep only a *few meaningful acceptance/smoke tests* for risky behaviour (genuine contacts, JAX array shape, sensor/actor parity, cold start and checkpoint resume). Do not expand tests purely to make the checklist look complete.
- A code review may identify unnecessary complexity and **remove it** even if it has tests. Tests protect essential semantics, not architectural clutter.
- Known CI problems must be documented, but do **not** make every work session a seven-minute CI-watch exercise. Prioritise reviewing/refactoring the actual implementation. Never start a huge GPU run on unverified physical contact or broken cold-start training.
- This changes **how** gates are completed: by small, understandable implementations and a convincing physical proof, not by counting test cases. It does not waive explicit review before touching `main`.

### G3 → G4 implementation decision (2026-10-23, owner asked to move beyond viewer)

**Build the next physical terrain work in the existing `EmuMJXEnv`, not in another trainer or a permanently parallel spike.** The standalone `terrain_spike.py` proved that batched `geom_pos` can distinguish raised and buried boxes (owner-observed foot/floor 1/1, foot/step 1/1 and rays 8/16); when production geometry is accepted, retire or shrink the duplicated spike to one focused acceptance entry point.

1. **One geometry authority, episode-varying severity.** A fixed-topology set of genuinely collidable finite pieces owns both the step-up and the step-down case (a step-down means leaving an actual *raised starting support*, not dropping below a fictitious world-zero datum). Geometry, approach direction and height must become per-world/per-episode state in the existing reset/physics path, without recompiling a topology for every difficulty change. Current `build_batched_model` bakes model variation at construction, and its disabled-domain early-return does not currently provide an episode-level terrain input. Resolve this explicitly before connecting `RegimeGovernor`; do not disguise host-only height changes as a growing curriculum.
2. **Collision proof before teaching.** Check foot/floor and foot/riser/upper-platform contacts at approach, near edge, just past edge, angle and reverse; check absence of ghost collision when terrain disabled. Keep real contact normals, world XY progression and uninterrupted episodes for evaluation. Probe teleportation/settling is geometry evidence ONLY, not evidence the policy walked a step.
3. **Actor measurements are distinct from examiner geometry.** The viewer's signed height currently uses world z=0; a raised support to zero-height exit therefore reads +5cm followed by 0cm, not an explicit -5cm drop. Fix this at the observation-semantic stage using *measured local support/relative depth*, NOT a privileged height-map or floor/foot-contact truth given to the actor. A vertical-looking-down camera may have blind edges, self-occlusions and invalid returns. Preserve raw ranges, ages and validity, and compare sensor-visible vs dimension-matched blinded policies.
4. **One bounded growth budget, not independent runaway dials.** The first 20M remains truly flat in *collision and sensor returns*. When healthy core competence has passed, allocate only a small share of training to terrain, advancing height/angle/exposure one frontier at a time and retreating failed terrain locally. Ordinary commands, posture, disturbances and efficiency remain practised. All curriculum inputs, sampled episode terrain and rollback/resume lineage must be versioned and reported; frozen examinations never control the teacher.
5. **One truthful evaluation protocol.** Report step-up/down requested-corridor completion with support, direction and no resets, alongside independent core command/recovery and grace metrics. Capture confidence/unknowns, baseline-vs-candidate regressions and actual throughput/VRAM before authorizing a foundation/fork campaign.

**Next code change:** extend the existing environment's *physical terrain model/state path* with a minimal episode-varied finite step and a corresponding diagnostic contact/approach proof. Do not add PPO rewards, sensor schema or governor settings until the geometry/contact/sensor ownership path is physically and computationally convincing. Keep `main`, champion and the accepted 220/232 checkpoint contracts untouched.
